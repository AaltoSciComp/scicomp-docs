Acknowledging RSE support
=========================

Acknowledgments are free-form and you can adapt the text to suit your
context - there are no strict requirements.  If you are at Aalto, your
publication (and other outputs) should be linked to the Science-IT
project (see :ref:`acris-link`).

If a RSE has contributions that meet the threshold for scientific
authorships, you should discuss co-authorships with them (hopefully
this was discussed at the beginning of the project).

If the authors wish to acknowledge RSEs:

   The authors wish to acknowledge the [scientific computing, or
   whatever it was] support provided by the Aalto University Research
   Software Engineer team.

or

   The authors wish to acknowledge the [scientific computing, or
   whatever it was] support provided by NAME of the Aalto University
   Research Software Engineer team.

If the authors wish to acknowledge our computational resources
(Triton, LLM gateway, etc.) (see also
:doc:`/triton/acknowledgingtriton`):

   The authors wish to acknowledge the computational resources
   provided by the Aalto Science-IT project.

If both:

   The authors wish to acknowledge the computational resources and the
   [scientific computing] support provided by the Aalto Science-IT
   project and the Aalto University Research Software Engineer team,
   respectively.
